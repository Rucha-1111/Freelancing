Terms & Conditions

Each site is a custom build around your business, content and branding — a one-page site in HTML/CSS/JS, or a multi-page site in React. Sections and functionality are agreed before development starts.

One-page websites start at ₹4,999. The final price depends on design complexity, content volume and functionality. Multi-page React builds are quoted separately and start above the one-page price. A fixed quote is shared once requirements are finalised.

50% advance is due to begin work; the remaining 50% is due before final source code and files are handed over. Development starts only after the advance is received, and the advance is non-refundable once development has begun.

Standard delivery is 5–7 business days after the advance is received, all content is provided, and requirements are finalised. Delays in content, images or feedback will extend the timeline. Larger, more custom projects may take longer — this is communicated upfront.

You provide business information, logo, brand assets, text, images, pricing details, contact info and any other content the site needs — and confirm you have the rights to use everything supplied.

Two rounds of reasonable revisions are included. A full change in design direction, new functionality, or a major scope change is treated as additional work and quoted separately.

Domain and hosting are not included and remain your responsibility to purchase and maintain. You'll receive the complete source code plus basic instructions for deploying it — no ongoing hosting or deployment service is provided.

Once final payment is received, you get the full source code and project files to host and use for your business. Any third-party fonts, libraries, plugins or services used remain subject to their own licences.

New pages, backend features, databases, booking systems, payment integrations, extra animations or major redesigns are quoted separately and only started with your approval.

If you cancel after development has started, the advance payment is non-refundable, and any significant additional work already completed may also be billed. Cancellations before development begins are discussed based on work already done.

Before delivery, you'll review the completed site and use your included revisions. Once revisions are done and final payment is received, the project is considered delivered — changes requested after that are treated as additional work.

Approving a quotation and paying the advance confirms you've reviewed and accepted the scope, pricing, timeline and terms outlined here.
